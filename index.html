Everything you need

All in one native app, designed for speed and simplicity.

Home

Install, update, launch, or uninstall Mocktail with isolated multi-instance slots for running multiple accounts.

FFlags Editor

Browse and edit flags confirmed to work on Roblox's allowlist, with built-in presets for quick configuration.

Settings

Configure Mocktail wrapper options through a clean GUI without touching config files manually.

Advanced Tools

Quick access to config/data folders, asset overlay management, and one-click resets.

Texture Overrides

Manage and import custom textures with a streamlined workflow designed for Mocktail.

Native & Lightweight

Built with Rust, GTK4, and libadwaita. No Electron, just a fast native binary.

Important Notes

  • Allowlist matters. FastFlags outside Roblox's allowlist do nothing — Lemonyde only lists ones confirmed to still work.
  • Use at your own risk. Running multiple instances at once is against Roblox's rules and can risk a flag on your account. Lemonyde warns about this in the app.
  • Unofficial project. Not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or bigrat.space.
